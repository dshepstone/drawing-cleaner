DRAWING CLEANER
Pencil drawings to clean lines on a transparent background.

TO START
  1. Right-click the zip you downloaded and choose "Extract All...".
  2. Open the extracted folder and double-click DrawingCleaner.exe.

IF WINDOWS SHOWS A BLUE "Windows protected your PC" BOX
  This appears because the app is new, not because anything is wrong.
  Click "More info", then click "Run anyway".

TO USE
  1. Choose folder...   Pick the folder that holds your drawings (PNG or JPG).
  2. Adjust if needed   Raise "Paper cleanup" if grey haze is left behind.
                        Raise "Line darkness" if your lines look too faint.
                        Hold Space to compare with the original.
  3. Click the blue button.

  The cleaned drawings are saved in a new folder beside yours with "_clean"
  added to its name. Your original drawings are never changed.

TO LOAD INTO OPENTOONZ
  Level > Load Level..., go to the "_clean" folder and pick the sequence.

Full instructions: https://github.com/dshepstone/drawing-cleaner
